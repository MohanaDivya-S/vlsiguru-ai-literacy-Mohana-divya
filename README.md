## Purpose of the AI Literacy Layer
The 16-week AI Literacy Layer teaches me to use AI tools responsibly in engineering work. The habit I am building is: ask, inspect, verify, document, and reflect. I do not accept AI output as true just because it sounds fluent. I check important claims against reliable sources and record what I found.
